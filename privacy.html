Data Retention & Candidate Purge Policy

All candidate inquiries and associated contact metadata are permanently purged from active triage within 90 days of resolution. Operational audit logs for security diagnostics are retained for 180 days.

To exercise your statutory right to data inspection or early purge, write directly to grievance@csodisha.com.
