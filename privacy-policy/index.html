Privacy Policy

Common Sense Odisha (CSO) · Draft for review · 30 October 2026

This page explains CSO's current approach and may be revised as website services develop.

Overview

Common Sense Odisha (CSO) is an independent information and learning website. This policy describes information handling associated with the current website features.

Browser storage

CSO uses browser local storage for certain preferences and interface features, and session storage for certain search-related functionality. These features operate within your browser. You can clear stored website data using your browser settings.

Community registration

The current Community registration form is a preview. It validates entered details in the browser but is not connected to a registration backend. The form currently does not submit or save registration details.

Email communications

If you email CSO, your message, email address and any information you choose to include may be received and retained in the CSO email account to respond to your enquiry. Avoid sharing passwords, identity documents or sensitive information unnecessarily.

Hosting and external services

CSO is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. Hosting and network services may process technical information necessary to deliver the website. External websites linked from CSO may have their own privacy practices, which CSO does not control.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when CSO features or data practices change. For privacy-related questions, contact [email protected].
