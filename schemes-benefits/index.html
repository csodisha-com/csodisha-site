    📋 Before You Apply: DBT Bank Seeding, e-District Certificates & Anti-Scam Checklist Expand guide

    1. NPCI Aadhaar Bank Seeding (DBT)

    Subhadra, CM-KISAN, Post-Matric Scholarship, and Madhu Babu Pension disburse strictly via the Aadhaar-linked NPCI mapper. Ask your bank branch for DBT Seeding / NPCI Consent or verify status at myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in ↗.

    2. e-District Odisha Certificates

    Keep fresh digital copies (valid within 1 year for Income) of Caste, Residence, and Family Income certificates ready through edistrict.odisha.gov.in ↗ or your nearest Mo Seba Kendra before scholarship deadlines.

    3. Official Portal & Zero-Fee Verification

    Common Sense Odisha only links to verified .gov.in / .nic.in official portals. Government DBT schemes never ask for OTPs, UPI PINs, or private agent commissions.
