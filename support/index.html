Report an Error

Accurate information matters. If you notice a broken link, outdated deadline, incorrect eligibility condition, or another problem, please note:

  • The page title and URL
  • The information that appears incorrect
  • A reliable official source supporting the correction, if available
Report by email: contact@csodisha.com. Include the affected page URL, a description of the issue, and an official source if available. Your email application will handle sending the report.

Suggest a Resource

CSO welcomes ideas for useful, accessible learning and public-information resources. Helpful suggestions may include:

  • Free study materials or official educational portals
  • Verified scholarship, recruitment or scheme resources
  • Accessibility or usability improvements
Suggest by email: contact@csodisha.com. Include the resource name, link, and why it may be useful. Your email application will handle sending the suggestion.

Contact Us

For general enquiries, website assistance, error reports, and resource suggestions, contact Common Sense Odisha by email.

CSO contact email: contact@csodisha.com. Please avoid sending passwords, identity documents, or other sensitive personal information unless genuinely necessary.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Common Sense Odisha?

CSO is an independent information and learning platform focused on helping people explore educational resources, opportunities, schemes and practical tools relevant to Odisha.

Is CSO an official government website?

No. CSO is an independent platform. For applications, eligibility, deadlines and final decisions, consult the relevant official authority.

Are the study resources free?

CSO provides a Free Study Library. Availability and usage conditions of linked third-party materials may vary; check the original source.

How do I verify an update?

Check the original official notification or issuing organisation, especially before submitting an application, paying a fee or sharing personal information.

How can I report an error or suggest a resource?

You can email contact@csodisha.com. Include the relevant page or resource link and a clear explanation. This website does not currently provide a submission form.
