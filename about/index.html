3-Step Editorial Verification Process

Every job notice, scheme rule, and exam notification passes three structured checks before publication.

STEP 01

Extract Official Gazette / PDF

Source vacancy counts, age limits, pay matrix, and eligibility clauses directly from primary .gov.in PDFs — never unverified rumours.

STEP 02

Cross-Verify Dates & Domains

Validate deadline dates, category relaxations, and official apply hostnames against our authentic portal allowlist.

STEP 03

Timestamp & Corrigendum Audit

Publish with clear verification timestamps and log citizen corrections transparently in our Public Changelog.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about CSO services, offline study tools, and citizen verification.

Is Common Sense Odisha an official government website?

No. Common Sense Odisha is an independent civic platform. We curate and explain public notices clearly and always route final submissions to official .gov.in or .nic.in websites.

Are all Mock Tests, PYQs, and Study Library guides 100% free?

Yes. There are zero subscriptions, hidden charges, or paywalled mock series on CSO.

Can I review saved flashcards and study notes offline?

Yes. Our Progressive Web App caches key shells and stores bookmarks, flashcards, and mistake notes in local IndexedDB so you can revise offline.

How is my personal data protected under the DPDP Act 2023?

CSO operates without third-party ad networks, stores only minimal account state required for sync, and lets you export or delete your account anytime.

How fast are citizen corrections reviewed?

Most evidence-backed reports sent via “Report an Error” or corrections@csodisha.com are verified against the primary gazette and updated within 24 hours.
