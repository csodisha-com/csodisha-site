1. NEP 2020 Foundational Stage & NIPUN Odisha (FLN)

The National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 replaces the 10+2 structure with 5+3+3+4. The Foundational Stage covers ages 3–8: 3 years of Anganwadi/Balvatika (ECCE) plus Classes 1 and 2, followed by Class 3 NIPUN Lakshya proficiency in Oral Language (କଥନ), Reading Fluency (ପଠନ — 45–60 words/min), Writing (ଲିଖନ), and Numeracy (ଗଣିତ up to 999).

2. OSEPA Class 1–5 Curriculum & OAVS Bridge

Odisha School Education Programme Authority (OSEPA) prescribes bilingual textbooks in Odia (Sahitya), English, Mathematics (Ganita), and Environmental Studies (Paribesh Vigyan), supported by Jaadui Pitara play kits, Kalika, Khelaghar, and Multilingual Education (MLE) in 21 tribal languages, preparing students for Odisha Adarsha Vidyalaya (OAVS) Class 6 admission.

3. ECCE, Anganwadi & Teacher Exam Notes (OTET / JT / Supervisor)

Covers Piaget's cognitive stages (Sensorimotor 0–2, Preoperational 2–7), Vygotsky's ZPD & scaffolding, Montessori & Froebel play-way methods, RPwD Act 2016 CWSN inclusion, ICDS / Mukhyamantri Sampoorna Pushti Yojana (MSPY) nutrition-learning convergence, and Holistic Progress Cards (HPC).
